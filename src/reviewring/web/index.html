Follow the reviewers. See what they share, and what is still unknown.

About the demo data

Every reviewer, business, rating and review page in this demo was invented for it. Nothing was collected from SerpApi or Google Maps, and nothing describes a real business or person. The demo is built to show two things side by side: a group of reviewers who share several unrelated businesses, and an everyday pair who both used a cafe and a grocer.

It runs through the same graph, overlap and expansion code as a real investigation, so what you see is how real observations would be presented. Its figures are not findings about anyone.

Real investigations need a SerpApi key on this computer

None is configured, so nothing new can be collected. Searches and businesses already saved here still work.

You do not need a key to explore the fictional demo.

How to set up a key

Copy .env.example to .env in the project folder, set SERPAPI_API_KEY to your own key, and restart ReviewRing. The steps are in README.md under "Optional: live investigation with your own SerpApi key". The key stays on this computer and is never sent to the browser.

A hosted public copy of ReviewRing is the fictional demo only: it has no Investigate tab and cannot run real investigations.

What these settings do

Each review page is one request. A further page is requested only if the previous page provides a next-page token, so fewer pages may be retrieved than you ask for.

Each history is one request for one reviewer's most recent reviews, up to the entry limit; the service may return fewer. Reviewers whose whole history should fit in that limit are chosen first, in page order, and those reporting a single review are skipped. A history retrieved with one entry limit is not reused for another.

              Coverage

              Filled bar: collected. Empty track: reported to exist, not collected. A full bar covers one business's reported reviews, not the whole investigation.

              Business ratings vs. collected reviews

              Google's rating covers every review of a business. The collected reviews are a small, unrepresentative sample, so no average is computed from them.

              ReviewRing shows structural patterns in who reviewed what. It does not decide whether any review, reviewer, or business is fraudulent, and nothing here is proof of wrongdoing.
