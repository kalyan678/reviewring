Follow the reviewers. See what they share, and what is still unknown.

Investigate a business

Search Google Maps through SerpApi, choose a business, then collect its first page of reviews and a few reviewers' review histories. Before anything is sent you are told the most it can cost, and nothing is sent until you confirm.

No SerpApi key is configured, so only searches and businesses already saved on this computer can be used. Add SERPAPI_API_KEY to the .env file and restart to collect new data.

Choose a business

Each history is one request for one reviewer's most recent reviews, up to the entry limit; the service may return fewer. Reviewers whose whole history should fit in that limit are chosen first, in page order, and those reporting a single review are skipped. A history retrieved with one entry limit is not reused for another.
