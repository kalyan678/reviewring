About the demo data

Every reviewer, business, rating and review page in this demo was invented for it. Nothing was collected from SerpApi or Google Maps, and nothing describes a real business or person. The demo is built to show two things side by side: a group of reviewers who share several unrelated businesses, and an everyday pair who both used a cafe and a grocer.

It runs through the same graph, overlap and expansion code as a real investigation, so what you see is how real observations would be presented. Its figures are not findings about anyone.

What these settings do

Each review page is one request. A further page is requested only if the previous page provides a next-page token, so fewer pages may be retrieved than you ask for.

Each history is one request for one reviewer's most recent reviews, up to the entry limit; the service may return fewer. Reviewers whose whole history should fit in that limit are chosen first, in page order, and those reporting a single review are skipped. A history retrieved with one entry limit is not reused for another.
